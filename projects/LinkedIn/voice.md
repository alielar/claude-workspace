# Voice and format (from Ali, 2026-09-27)

## Who and why
- English only.
- Readers: students, business owners, founders, thinkers, creative people who like to think about where AI is taking us.
- Goal: Ali becomes "the AI guy". Someone genuinely into AI who shares clear insights on what is going on now.
- Ali is not a founder. No "lessons I learned as a founder". He shares insights and observations.

## How it sounds
- Casual, but respectful of LinkedIn. Never stiff, never bro.
- Storytelling with a structure: a hook, a short narrative, a point, a question.
- Short paragraphs of one or two sentences with a blank line between them (Jes Black style).
- Plain words. Explain every technical thing in one sentence a non-technical reader gets.
- Contrarian takes are welcome when honest.

## Rules
- 12 to 20 short lines. Go into the story and its implications, not just the headline.
- No emojis. No hashtags.
- Opens with the signature line, always with "Hello friends" and a variation: "Hello friends, coming to share a thought", "Hello friends, I have another thought for you today", "Hello friends, coming back with a thought about ...".
- Ends with an open invitation, not a question: "Don't hesitate to let me know what you think, or if you have doubts about this", with variations.
- Keep the company and its tools vague. Well-known tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot) can be named.
- Own automations: describe the intent and what changed, not the technical details.
- Each post gets a picture suggestion (free stock photo search or a screenshot idea).

## Mix per batch of 8
- 5 posts: AI news explained simply.
- 1 to 2 posts: an automation Ali built, told through the intent behind it.
- 1 to 2 posts: opinion on how work and the world are changing.

## Schedule
- Twice a week, Tuesday and Thursday, 7:00 in the morning.
- Ali schedules them himself in LinkedIn's built-in scheduler (up to 3 months ahead).
