The closing ladder

This is the one genuine sequence in your account, and it's good. It runs on a single day: the place is offered in the morning, and the deadline lands that evening. The pressure escalates deliberately — soft check-in, third-party pressure, hard deadline, then the place is reassigned.

Send these in order. Skipping to the deadline without the soft steps is what makes scarcity read as a bluff.

Stage 1

The place is yours

Morning, after the consultation
admission_message_fraMarketing
{name} · {owned_by_name}
Nous avons le plaisir de vous informer que vous faites partie des personnes sélectionnées pour rejoindre notre académie.

Frames enrolment as selection, not purchase. Sets up every later message — you can only lose a place you were told you'd won.

to_be_converted1_fraMarketing
{name}
Avez-vous des questions avant 20h ? Prévoyez-vous d'accepter la place ?

Plants the 20h deadline early and casually, so the evening messages are a reminder rather than a new demand.

Stage 2

Someone else is asking about you

Early afternoon
to_be_converted2_fraMarketing
Nous sommes en train de finaliser l'organisation d'aujourd'hui. Comment est la situation de votre côté ?

A reason to write that isn't "have you decided". The pressure is logistical, not personal.

to_be_converted3_fraMarketing
L'administration vient de me demander des nouvelles vous concernant. Pourriez-vous me répondre rapidement ?

Moves the pressure to a third party, so you stay on the lead's side. The strongest psychological move in the whole ladder.

Stage 3

The deadline is real

Late afternoon
tbc_followup1_fraMarketingBest in class
{name}
Où en êtes-vous dans votre réflexion ? Votre place est réservée jusqu'à ce soir 20h00. Prévoyez-vous de l'accepter ?

Named hour, named consequence, open question. If you keep only one closing template, keep this one.

to_be_converted6_fraMarketing
Si vous souhaitez commencer avec nous, vous devez me le confirmer aujourd'hui. Ou bien ne prévoyez-vous pas de participer ?

The negative alternative does the work — it's easier to reject a wrong assumption than to make a decision.

Stage 4

The place goes to someone else

Evening, at the deadline
to_be_converted4_fraMarketing
{name}
Aujourd'hui, je peux encore vous inscrire pour les prochains mois. Après cela, il ne sera de nouveau possible de rejoindre les cours qu'à partir de septembre/octobre au plus tôt, via la liste d'attente.

Quantifies the cost of waiting in months, not vague urgency. Update the month names each intake or it stops being credible.

Three things to fix

Your leads don't know who they're talking to

Twelve French templates introduce the sender as Léa, two as Juliette, two as Ali, and one as Paula — all from the same WhatsApp number. A lead who gets a booking message from Léa and a closing message from Ali sees an inconsistency, and inconsistency reads as a scam.

Several templates already solve this with an {owner_name} variable — fr_upsell_meeting_* and fr_revivingoldleads_3 among them. Make that the standard for every new template and the name always matches whoever actually replies.

You're paying Marketing rates for Utility messages

76 of your 116 French templates are Marketing category, which costs more per send and requires marketing opt-in. Some of that is unavoidable — an offer is an offer. But reminders, confirmations, payment chases and logistics are legitimately Utility, and you've already proved it: to_be_converted5_fra and payment_reminder_1_fr_new are approved as Utility while carrying real closing weight.

When you next resubmit a template, try Utility first. Worth knowing that the four templates in the campaign now running were submitted as Utility and Meta reclassified them to Marketing — so the content has to genuinely be transactional, not just labelled that way.

Naming is costing you time

Three conventions run in parallel — _fr, _fra, and _france — plus version suffixes _v1, _v2, _v3, _new, and _test used inconsistently. And the four templates in today's campaign misspell "meeting" as no_meating and hadmeating, which can't be corrected without resubmitting to Meta.

Pick one pattern for anything new — stage_purpose_fr_v1 — and the list stays navigable as it grows past 116.
