Set up Love

Five minutes, on this iPhone, in Safari. Do the steps in order.

  1. Install two free apps

    Scriptable shows the photo widget. ntfy shows a photo the second it is sent.

    Get Scriptable Get ntfy
  2. Turn on photo notifications

    Copy your private channel, open ntfy, tap +, paste it as the topic, tap Subscribe, and allow notifications.

    …

  3. Add the widget code to Scriptable

    Tap Copy. Open Scriptable, tap + at the top right, paste, then tap the title at the top and name it Love. Tap Done.

  4. Put the widget on your Home Screen

    Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit then Add Widget. Search Scriptable, swipe to the large size, tap Add. Then touch and hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, and set Script to Love.

  5. Add the Love app to your Home Screen

    This is where you send photos and see them all. Open it, tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.

    Open Love
  6. Optional: send from the Photos app

    Adds Send to Love to the Share menu, so you can send any photo from Photos in two taps. Tap, then Add Shortcut.

    Add Send to Love
